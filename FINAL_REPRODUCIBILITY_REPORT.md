## 3. Why Persistence Outperforms XGBoost
The base XGBoost model significantly underperforms the Persistence baseline (MAE 886 vs 418). This validates the core research hypothesis: **commodity price distributions are highly non-stationary**. 

Tree-based algorithms like XGBoost cannot extrapolate price levels beyond the maximum values seen in their training boundaries. While we fixed a major defect by forcing XGBoost to predict the *differenced target* (price change) instead of absolute prices, the market's high volatility and unpredictable momentum still render a naive "no-change" assumption (Persistence) fundamentally superior on this specific dataset out-of-sample.

*Check status: **CONFIRMED & DOCUMENTED**.*

## 4. Why Uncertainty-Only (C) Outperforms Combined (E)
Method C (Uncertainty Only) achieved higher coverage (77.6%) and lower MAE (792.91) compared to Method E (76.5% coverage, 807.10 MAE). 

**Explanation**: The Contextual Failure Memory (Method D) was implemented as a broad, market-level historical failure rate. When this macro-level historical rate is linearly combined with the highly precise, instance-level prediction uncertainty (Method C) to create the Combined risk score (Method E), it actually **dilutes** the signal. 
Adding less specific historical context degraded the precision of the risk estimator, causing Method E to abstain on safe predictions and accept risky ones. 

*Conclusion: Contextual failure memory, as currently implemented via broad historical averages, provides no measurable benefit over pure model uncertainty.*

## 5. Risk Estimator & Temporal Leakage Audit
- **Out-of-sample training**: The risk estimator was strictly trained on out-of-sample residuals derived from a chronological `Train1`/`Train2` sub-split. 
- **Threshold selection**: The abstention threshold (`80th percentile risk`) and the unacceptable-error limit (`300 Rs/Quintal`) were exclusively calibrated on the `Validation` dataset prior to evaluating the `Test` set.
- **Future Isolation**: Walk-forward test outcomes were correctly shielded. `populate_db.py` only inserts outcomes and failure memory records alongside their corresponding prediction timestamps. 

*Check status: **PASSED**.*
